01 / ENGINEER TO ENGINEER

Send your list.
This is how we read it.

We do not judge by the designation alone. Confirmed conditions go on the quotation; questions and deviations go on the C&D list.

  1. 01
    Reading the specification

    Standard, material, grade and thread are checked line by line. Anything unclear comes back as a question.

  2. 02
    Mixed lists on one sheet

    BOMs mixing bolts, nuts, washers and special materials are accepted as they are. No need to ask several places separately.

  3. 03
    English RFQs

    English BOMs and RFQs are read line by line; questions and deviations are collected on the C&D list.

  4. 04
    Required documents

    We ask suppliers whether the required documents, such as mill certificates, can be provided, and state the result in the quotation.

03 / START WITH YOUR LIST

Start with
the documents you have.

BOMs, RFQs, drawings and photos, as they are. No need to send the same request to several places.

  1. Check the documents — each line is read, and missing specifications are asked in one go.
  2. Confirm with suppliers — once we have your answers, we confirm the conditions with suppliers.
  3. Quotation — confirmed conditions go on the quotation; unconfirmed conditions and deviations go on the C&D list. Lines we cannot supply are listed with the reason.
