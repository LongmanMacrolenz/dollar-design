Who this is for

Engineers and buyers at foreign-owned plants, EPC sites and maintenance contractors in Korea who work with English BOMs and ASTM / ASME / ISO / DIN designations, and who need a Korean supplier that reads them line by line.

Metric and inch

Hex bolts and nuts, socket screws, washers, threaded rod, pins and rivets in metric sizes; inch UNC/UNF and 8UN fasteners, including ASTM A193 / A194 / A320 studs with heavy hex nuts for flanged joints.

Read line by line

Each line is checked for standard and edition, size and pitch, length basis, grade, coating and required documents. Missing specifications are collected into one question list instead of being guessed.

Told up front

We hold no stock: every order is sourced from suppliers in Korea. Prices and lead times are written only after the supplier confirms them. Lines we cannot supply are listed with the reason.

How it works

  1. Send — e-mail the BOM, RFQ, drawing or photo in English. No form to fill in.
  2. Line check — a mechanical engineer reads every line against the stated standard and grade.
  3. Questions — open points (length basis, coating, certificates …) come back as one list.
  4. Quotation — a quotation in KRW with a clarification & deviation sheet and a list of lines that cannot be supplied.
