ASME B16.5

ASMEStandard

Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings: NPS 1/2 Through NPS 24 Metric/Inch Standard

← Back to topicSend a BOM or RFQ →
Key pointsBuying checksBasis

Standard for pressure-temperature ratings, materials, dimensions, tolerances, marking, and testing of NPS 1/2 to 24 pipe flanges and flanged fittings. It also has a bolting materials list, a drilling template (bolt count and diameter), and an appendix for calculating stud length.

Status: Current

Key points

  • Pressure Classes 150, 300, 400, 600, 900, and 1500 (NPS 1/2 to 24) and 2500 (NPS 1/2 to 12). NPS 26 and above is ASME B16.47.
  • It has a table of applicable bolting materials and a table of recommended flange bolting dimensions (Tables 1B and 1C in the 2017 edition's table of contents).
  • The stud bolt length calculation method is in Appendix C (2017 edition's table of contents).

Checks for BOMs and buying

  • B16.5 Class and the PN (EN 1092-1) and K (JIS B 2220) ratings are different systems. Check the bolt circle, bolt count, and diameter separately in each standard's tables.
  • Pump and equipment nozzle flanges may be thicker than standard, so check the stud length against the equipment drawing.
  • RTJ face flanges may have longer studs than RF of the same NPS and Class. If the face type changes, recheck the length.

Equivalent and superseding standards

  • EN 1092-1 European flange of the PN system (separate system)
  • JIS B 2220 K-rating flange (separate system)
