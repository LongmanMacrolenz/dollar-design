ASTM F3125/F3125M

ASTMStandard

High Strength Structural Bolts and Assemblies, Steel and Alloy Steel, Heat Treated, Inch Dimensions 120 ksi, 144 ksi, and 150 ksi Minimum Tensile Strength, and Metric Dimensions 830 MPa and 1040 MPa Minimum Tensile Strength

Key pointsTableBuying checksBasis

Specification for high-strength bolts for steel structure connections. In 2015 the six standards A325, A325M, A490, A490M, F1852, and F2280 were combined into one and retained as grades.

Status: Current (established 2015 · A325 · A325M · A490 · A490M · F1852 · F2280 withdrawn in 2016 and consolidated as grades)

Key points

  • Grades: A325 and A325M (120 ksi, 830 MPa), A490 and A490M (150 ksi, 1040 MPa), and twist-off types F1852 (120 ksi) and F2280 (150 ksi). The 2025 edition added the inch-only Grade 144 (144 ksi).
  • The types are heavy hex and twist-off. Sizes: A325 and A490 are 1/2" to 1-1/2" (M12 to M36); F1852, F2280, and Grade 144 are 1/2" to 1-1/4".
  • Type 1 is medium carbon steel, carbon boron steel, or alloy steel (including boron-added); Type 3 is weathering steel. The head carries a grade mark and a manufacturer mark.
  • F3125 Grade A325 is the 120 ksi class (Group 120). The former A325 was divided into 120/105 ksi depending on diameter.
  • A490 (Group 150) cannot be hot-dip or mechanically galvanized. The current RCSC Specification (2025) allows the zinc flake coating ASTM F3393 (F1136, F2833, and F3019, which the 2020 edition listed, were replaced by F3393 in 2025).
  • A325 can be hot-dip (F2329) or mechanically (B695 Class 55) galvanized; in that case the nut is overtapped and lubricated, and a rotational capacity test is run on the matched bolt and nut.
  • Mating parts: nut ASTM A563 DH (for uncoated A325 Type 1, C, C3, D, and DH3 are also possible; for Type 3, C3 and DH3 for A325 and DH3 for A490; the A194 2H heavy nut is allowed instead of C, D, and DH), washer ASTM F436. Dimensions follow ASME B18.2.6.

Checks for BOMs and buying

  • Read "A325" and "A490" on drawings as "F3125 Grade A325 and A490" now. The standard number changed; it is not a different product.
  • A BOM that specifies hot-dip or mechanical galvanizing for A490 or F2280 violates the RCSC Specification, so check the coating.
  • Structural bolts have a shorter threaded portion than ordinary heavy hex bolts. Do not replace them with SAE Grade 5 or 8 bolts or B18.2.1 bolts.
  • For galvanized A325, bolts and nuts are plated in the same process, and the rotational capacity test records of the matched assembly are obtained.
  • The Korean standards for the same use are the KS B 1010 (F10T) and KS B 2819 (S10T) sets; their strength systems and dimensions differ and they are not interchangeable. F3125 is specified on US-designed drawings.

Equivalent and superseding standards

  • EN 14399 series European high-strength structural bolt set (thread and dimension systems differ)
  • KS B 1010 (F10T) · KS B 2819 (S10T) Korean friction-grip high-strength bolt set (same use, separate standard and strength system)
