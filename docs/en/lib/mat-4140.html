AISI 4140 · 4142 (Cr-Mo steel)

CommonMaterial

AISI/SAE 4140 and 4142 Chromium-Molybdenum Alloy Steel

← Back to topicSend a BOM or RFQ →
Key pointsBuying checksBasis

A family of medium carbon low-alloy steels containing Cr and Mo. JIS G 4107 lists SAE 4140, 4142, and 4145 as the representative steel types for bolt material SNB7 (A193 B7 noted for reference).

Key points

  • JIS G 4107 SNB7 composition (revision draft): C 0.38–0.48, Mn 0.75–1.00, Cr 0.80–1.10, Mo 0.15–0.25 %.
  • JIS G 4053 SCM440 composition: C 0.38–0.43, Mn 0.60–0.90, Cr 0.90–1.20, Mo 0.15–0.30 % - lower Mn range and higher Cr range than SNB7.
  • A320 notes that because of hardenability the maximum diameter at which the required properties can be obtained may be limited for some grades, and also has grade L43, a low-temperature grade of the AISI 4340 family with added Ni.
  • Corresponding steel type: JIS G 4053 SCM440. Bar stock for bolts is JIS G 4107 SNB7 (KS D 3755 of the same name has been withdrawn).

Checks for BOMs and buying

  • "4140 bolt" is only a material name, not a standard grade - specify with grades such as B7 and L7 that have test and marking requirements.
  • When making SNB7 or B7 bolts from SCM440 bar stock, confirm with the mill certificate that the composition of each heat is within the range of the bolt material in question (by JIS, the Mn and Cr ranges of SCM440 and SNB7 differ).

Equivalent and superseding standards

  • SCM440 (JIS G 4053) Corresponding steel type - with Mn 0.60–0.90 and Cr 0.90–1.20 % it is not the same as the SNB7 composition range
