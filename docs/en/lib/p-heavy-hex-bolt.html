Heavy hex bolt

CommonPart

Heavy Hex Bolt / Heavy Hex Screw

← Back to topicSend a BOM or RFQ →
Key pointsTableBuying checksBasis

A bolt whose head width across flats is larger than that of a hex bolt of the same nominal size. For inch general use it is ASME B18.2.1 heavy hex, for structural use ASME B18.2.6 + ASTM F3125, and metric mainly comes from structural set standards.

Key points

  • Even among heavy hex, the bolt type (heavy hex bolt) and the screw type (heavy hex screw) are treated in separate dimension tables in ASME B18.2.1.
  • ASTM A307 Grade B is a grade for cast iron flange piping joints, and its default mating nut is A563 Grade A heavy hex (A307-21).
  • Structural heavy hex bolts (F3125 A325 and A490) have ASME B18.2.6 dimensions, a shorter threaded portion than general-purpose bolts, and the same head width across flats as the heavy nut (RCSC 2025).
  • ISO general hex bolts (ISO 4014 and 4017) have the normal-series width across flats, and metric bolts with a larger width across flats come mainly from structural standards (EN 14399, ISO 7412 withdrawn in 2006).

Checks for BOMs and buying

  • For lines with only "HEAVY HEX BOLT", confirm whether it is structural (F3125 A325 and A490) or general (A307 Grade B and A354), and whether it is bolt type or screw type.
  • Do not substitute SAE Grade 5 and 8 hex cap screws for structural bolts. Thread length, mating nut and test requirements differ.
  • Heavy bolts are usually paired with heavy nuts. State the nut shape (hex, heavy) and grade together.
  • For metric "heavy bolt" notation, the width across flats basis differs by standard (example: EN 14399-4 HV M20 is 32 mm). Match to the dimensions of the stated standard.
