Hex bolt (Hex bolt · Hex cap screw)

CommonPart

Hexagon Head Bolt / Hexagon Head Screw / Hex Cap Screw

← Back to topicSend a BOM or RFQ →
Key pointsTableBuying checksBasis

In a BOM, "hex bolt" maps to ISO 4014 (partially threaded) and ISO 4017 (fully threaded) for metric, and to hex bolt and hex cap screw of ASME B18.2.1 for inch. Thread, length, strength and surface treatment must be stated together to make an order specification.

Key points

  • Metric hex bolts divide into partially threaded ISO 4014 (formerly DIN 931) and fully threaded ISO 4017 (formerly DIN 933). KS B 1002 and JIS B 1180 are national standards of the same family.
  • In the titles of ISO 4014 (hexagon head bolts) and ISO 4017 (hexagon head screws), "bolt" refers to partially threaded and "screw" to fully threaded.
  • ASME B18.2.1 treats hex bolt and hex cap screw as separate products with separate dimension tables. This is different from the partially/fully threaded distinction.
  • Length L is measured from the underside of the head (bearing face) to the end of the thread (ISO 4014 Figure 1).
  • Thread length b of ISO 4014 partially threaded bolts is 2d + 6 for l ≤ 125 mm, 2d + 12 for 125 < l ≤ 200, and 2d + 25 mm for l > 200.
  • Strength is specified by ISO 898-1 for metric steel (4.6, 4.8, 8.8, 10.9, etc.), ISO 3506-1 for stainless steel (A2-70, A4-80, etc.), and SAE J429 Grade 2, 5, 8 or ASTM A307, A449, A354 for inch.
  • ISO 4014 and 4017 and DIN 931 and 933 differ in width across flats for M10, M12, M14 and M22: ISO 16, 18, 21, 34 mm; DIN 17, 19, 22, 32 mm.

Even for equivalent standards, dimensions, strength and test requirements are not all identical.

Checks for BOMs and buying

  • Ask back, together with the length, for lines without "partially threaded" or "fully threaded" notation. Below the standard minimum length no partially threaded bolt exists, so it comes as fully threaded (example: M12 partially threaded starts from 50 mm).
  • Do not translate English "HEX CAP SCREW" as "fully threaded". For inch cap screws too, partially threaded is the default when long.
  • If an ISO product is sent against a line marked DIN 931 or 933, the tool size changes for M10, M12, M14 and M22.
  • For inch lines without a thread count, do not assume UNC; confirm. UNC and UNF do not mate.
  • For zinc electroplating or black zinc plating on 10.9 and above, check on the certificate whether hydrogen embrittlement baking was done.
