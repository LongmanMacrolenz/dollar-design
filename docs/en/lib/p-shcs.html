Socket head cap screw (hex socket head bolt)

CommonPart

Hexagon Socket Head Cap Screw

← Back to topicSend a BOM or RFQ →
Key pointsTableBuying checksBasis

A bolt with a hex socket in a cylindrical head. Specified by metric ISO 4762 (DIN 912, KS B 1003, JIS B 1176) and inch ASME B18.3 + ASTM A574 (alloy steel) and F837 (stainless steel).

Key points

  • Length L is measured from the underside of the head to the end.
  • Metric property classes are 8.8, 10.9 and 12.9, and ISO 4762:1997 Table 2 sets the thread tolerance of 12.9 at 5g6g and the others at 6g.
  • ASME B18.3 retains the dimensions of the old 1936 series socket head cap screws in a separate appendix, so check the 1936 series on old drawings separately from the current dimensions.

Checks for BOMs and buying

  • Electro-galvanizing of 12.9 has a high hydrogen embrittlement risk, so black oxide is the default. If plating is needed, check the baking conditions on the certificate or switch to zinc flake.
  • "CAP SCREW" alone in an inch BOM does not tell whether it is a hex cap screw (hex head) or a socket head cap screw, so ask back.
  • For lines with a sour service requirement (NACE MR0175/ISO 15156), first check the hardness suitability of high-strength socket head cap screws (A574, 12.9).
  • M14 is a non-preferred (in parentheses) size in ISO 4762, and M18 and M22 are not in the ISO 4762 table.
