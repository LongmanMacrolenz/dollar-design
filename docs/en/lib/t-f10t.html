F10T · S10T · F8T (structural high-strength)

KSGrade

Structural high-strength bolt grades F8T, F10T, S10T

← Back to topicSend a BOM or RFQ →
Key pointsTableBuying checksBasis

Grade markings of high-strength bolt sets for steel structures in Korea and Japan. F is the friction grip hex set, S is the torque-shear type set, and the number means the lower limit of tensile strength (× 100 MPa).

Key points

  • Tensile strength by Japanese standards: F8T 800~1000, F10T · S10T 1000~1200 MPa. Proof strength (yield) is F8T 640, F10T · S10T 900 MPa or more.
  • F8T · F10T are the grade markings of KS B 1010 · JIS B 1186. S10T is the marking of the torque-shear type set; in Korea the KS B 2819 set is called this, and in Japan it is an association standard such as JSS II 09.
  • F10T specifies tensile strength as a range up to the upper limit, while ISO 10.9 specifies a minimum of 1040 MPa. They are not the same grade.
  • Japanese road bridges have not used F11T and above as new since 1980 because delayed fracture occurred. KS B 1010 added the 1300 MPa class F13T together with the delayed fracture test in the 2004 revision.
  • In the Japanese building field, there are the ultra-high-strength bolts F14T · S14T certified by the Minister of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (tensile strength 1400, proof strength 1260 MPa or more).

Checks for BOMs and buying

  • Do not replace an F10T set with general 10.9 hex bolts and nuts. The strength and torque coefficient requirements per set differ.
  • S10T and F10T sets have different tightening methods and are not interchanged.
  • The higher the strength, the more sensitive to hydrogen delayed fracture. Avoid pickling, electroplating, and corrosive environments, and if plating is needed, decide it at the design stage.
  • For a BOM that only says "HTB", check whether it is an F10T set or S10T, and the nominal size and length.

Equivalent and superseding standards

  • EN 14399-4 (HV 10.9) European set for the same use, not the same grade
  • ASTM F3125 Grade A490 Inch structural, not the same grade
