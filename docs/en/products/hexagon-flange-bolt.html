Hexagon Flange Bolt

Core familyCheck metric and inch designations

A hex nut with a disc (flange) acting as a washer on the bearing face. Specified by ISO 4161 and EN 1661 (formerly DIN 6923), KS B 1043, and for inch ASME B18.2.2, and the presence of serrations is decided together.

Differences to check before buying

  • The disc (flange) under the head acts as a washer, so sheet metal and covers are fastened without a washer.
  • Even for the same nominal size, the width across flats differs by series. Example: M8 is 13 mm for heavy (DIN 6921, EN 1665) and 10 mm for small (ISO 4162).
  • A serrated (toothed) bearing face resists loosening but scratches painted surfaces and thin plates. If the mating material is soft, choose a plain bearing face.
  • FLANGE BOLT in an English BOM often means a stud (B7, 2H) for a piping flange. If a grade or NUTS is noted, treat it as a stud.
